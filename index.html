01 / ABOUT
WELCOME TO ROBOKRITI

Where ideas become machines.

RoboKriti is an inter-class robotics competition created for the students of Army Public School, Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg, Lucknow.

Bringing together students from Classes 6 to 12, RoboKriti turns engineering, creativity, strategy and teamwork into an energetic technology battle.
